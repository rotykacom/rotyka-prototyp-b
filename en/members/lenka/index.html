People of Rotyka

Lenka

  • Thirty Years’ War armour
  • Thirty Years’ War

She began in the LA-MA historical fencing group and studied fencing at Lars’s First Prague Fencing Hall. In 1999, she became a founding member of Rotyka. She later attended courses and classes at the Magistérium fencing school under M. Khun.

In addition to fencing, she practises historical archery.

Her historical interests include the late Middle Ages, particularly the mid-15th century, and the Thirty Years’ War in the first half of the 17th century.
