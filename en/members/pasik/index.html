Petr, known as Pašík

  • Thirty Years’ War

A boyhood dream and an interest in historical weapons brought him to Rotyka in 2014. As the oldest member by age and the newest by length of service, he focuses on the Thirty Years’ War.

Besides fencing, he practises both historical and more modern forms of archery.
