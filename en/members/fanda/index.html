People of Rotyka

Fanda

  • Late Middle Ages
  • Thirty Years’ War
  • Equipment
  • Napoleonic Wars
  • The Hradec Court of Elizabeth Richeza

He has practised fencing since 1996, initially completing four years of armed self-defence training with Lars, later known as the First Prague Fencing Hall. From 1998, he also spent five years studying fencing there. In 2002, he began training with the hand-and-a-half sword at the Magistérium fencing school under Mirek Khun.

Between 1997 and 1999, he was a member of the LA-MA historical fencing group, and in 1999 he became a founding member of Rotyka. He also takes part in the Hradec Court of Queen Elizabeth Richeza project, portraying the elder “František z Šešovic”, and is a member of the Imperial and Royal 8th Hussar Regiment. He completed two years of basic horse-riding training with Lenka Šestáková and holds a firearms licence.

Weapons he uses include the knife, falchion, one-handed sword, axe, shield, hand-and-a-half sword, rapier, rapier and dagger, sabre, and both short and long firearms.

History & heraldry

The Lords of Hrdlořezy

The Lords of Hrdlořezy are a fictional family that I chose in connection with my birthplace. For my coat of arms, I chose a shield divided vertically: on the heraldic right, three black bars on a white field; on the left, half a red Atlantic cross issuing from the dividing line on a blue field.
