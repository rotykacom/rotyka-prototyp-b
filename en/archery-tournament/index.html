A look back at the fourteenth edition

Original report from 2026, translated into English. References to the next edition belong to the report and do not announce a confirmed date.

Archery tournament 2026 — results

On 3 October 2026, we held the 14th edition, in its modern history, of our archery tournament, in a medieval spirit using so-called primitive bows. Once again the weather was kind: sunshine throughout the day, as warm as summer, and clear blue skies made the tournament fly by. A total of 24 competitors gathered this year, including our loyal regulars, a few complete newcomers and several spectators.

What did we shoot at? Twelve events were divided into three themes, mostly involving 3D targets or an additional challenge, including two moving targets: hunting, with five targets; combat, with five targets; and two historical English longbow events. We enjoyed a pleasant day with bows in hand, followed by an evening together. We also scored the individual categories, but the tournament’s main prize and trophy went to the overall winner with the highest combined score.

The honorary title for the best hunter, “goddess Artemis”, went to Dalibor z Kozojed. This time, for the first time in the tournament’s history, the best fighter’s honorary title, “Attila”, was shared by two archers: Pavel H. and Dalibor z Kozojed! The master longbow archer’s title, “Robin Hood”, stayed at home this year, going to Mirek thanks to a solid performance in the Clout. Once again, however, at least one archer hit the Target at 100 yards!

Who was the overall winner? The battle for first place remained exciting to the very end. Second and third place were shared, again for the first time in the tournament’s history, and the final gap to first place was seven points. Huge congratulations: this year’s honorary title of “King of the Elves” goes once again to Dalibor!

As has become traditional, this year’s 14th edition ended with a lavish shared feast prepared from historical recipes by our own František, who once again did an amazing job ;) We firmly hope to hold the next, 15th edition in a similar spirit next year and look forward to a strong turnout. There might even be a little preliminary round in spring…

Many thanks to everyone who came. We trust that no one regretted taking part, including the pleasant evening spent over good food and drink :)

If you have any comments, suggestions, complaints or compliments :), please do not hesitate to send them to rotyka@seznam.cz. The same applies to reviews and photographs; we will be happy to publish worthwhile contributions on our website.

For Rotyka, Mirek

Archery tournament 2026
Additional information and rules

Archived rules and entry fees for 2026. The organisers will confirm the conditions of the next edition.

01EquipmentBows, arrows and historical clothing: what to bring.+

Bow: a traditional, or “primitive”, bow:

Any bow made using traditional technology from natural materials. Modern adhesives are permitted to the extent required. Traditional bows include originals or replicas of bows used throughout human history, including those of present-day Indigenous peoples, but exclude bows of modern construction made from modern materials. Bows may be made from a single piece or assembled from different natural materials.

Bowstring: a string made from natural materials may be used. For safety reasons, strings made from modern materials are also permitted.

Arrows: only arrows with shafts made from natural materials, primarily wood, but also reed and similar materials. Target points, including modern ones, are permitted, as are forged bodkin and Tudor points and simple flat hunting points without barbs. The point must not prevent the arrow from being easily removed from the target. Fletching must also be natural: feathers.

Binding or stitching the fletching is optional. We recommend binding at least the front end, and possibly the rear end as well, but it is not strictly required. Nocks must be made from natural materials, such as wood or horn, or cut into the shaft. If a simple self-nock without an insert is used, binding at the nock is required for safety.

The whole tournament, across all events, must be shot with a single bow. The only exception is a technical failure, at the organiser’s discretion. Arrows may be changed.

Clothing: historical dress that meets at least a basic presentable standard and matches the style of bow is very welcome, but is not compulsory. Historical clothing is not a condition of entry.

02EventsThe three categories and the main tournament entry fee.+

The tournament is divided into three interconnected themes: English longbow, combat and hunting. See below for a more detailed description of the events and targets.

Entry fee: CZK 350, or CZK 150 for juniors, covering the entire main tournament.

03ScoringHits, scores, tie-breaks and tournament titles.+

An arrow that cuts the boundary of a scoring area, a “line-cutter”, always scores the higher value if it breaks the line. Merely touching or partly covering the line is not enough.

An arrow must stick into the target or pass through it, including at its edge. An arrow that rebounds from the target does not count, except for certain targets, such as Clout. An arrow that hits after rebounding from an obstacle, such as a tree or the ground, and sticks in point-first scores normally.

If competitors are tied for any of the top three places overall, or for first place in a section of the tournament, there will be a shoot-off at a target specified by the organiser.

The results of each section will be announced separately, with a small prize and the honorary titles “Robin Hood” for English longbow, “Artemis” for hunting, and “Attila” for combat. The overall ranking is the sum of all events in all three sections. The overall winner receives the tournament trophy as the main prize, the honorary title “King of the Elves”, and their name permanently inscribed on the travelling trophy.
